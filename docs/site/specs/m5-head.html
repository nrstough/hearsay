The M5 head on XLS-R

A trained head on the first twelve layers of XLS-R: learned layer weights, attentive statistics pooling and a linear output, trained on rented A100s with heavy class-blind augmentation. It failed its gate as a replacement for the probe and ships as a fifth of the fusion vote, because it errs on different files.

In plain words

The plan for M5A trained head on a truncated XLS-R: learned layer weights, attentive pooling and a linear output, trained on rented GPUs with heavy augmentation. It did not beat the plain probe on its own and ships as a minority vote in the fusion. was to fine-tune XLS-R and beat the frozen probe. Fine-tuning the kept layers fit the seen generators and transferred worse to the unseen one, so the shipped arm keeps the backbone frozen and trains only a small head over the hidden states. On its own that head lost to the probe on the holdout, on LibriSpeech real speech and on short clips, and won only on In-the-Wild false alarms. The pre-declared M5 sweep then showed that as a minority vote beside the probe and the handcrafted model it improves every readout, which is the role it ships in.

What goes in and comes out

In: trimmed, band-limited, capped and normalized audio (the deploy transform). Out: one logit, high for synthetic, mapped to a probability for the sentence "XLS-R fine-tuned head (M5, 12 layers, attentive pooling): logit +x.xx (synthetic-like, P=0.xx)". Export column m5_xlsr_ft.

Where it lives

Model in m5_model.py; data, folds and the deploy transform in m5_data.py; augmentation in augment.py; the bundle in m5_bundle.py; trainer, scorer and assembler in scripts/m5_*.py; the vast.ai launch and reaper scripts under scripts/cloud/; the runner's path in pipeline.py, lines 485–495.

Known limits

The final encoder LayerNorm and the mask embedding were unintentionally trainable in the shipped run (a few thousand parameters); fixed afterwards and pinned by a test. The M5 addendum's first In-the-Wild run scored whole clips instead of the shared crops and was repeated crop-fair; both are reported. On MLAAD its miss rate is the in-sample one.

Tests that pin it

tests/test_m5_model.py (frozen and trainable sets with a counterfactual, padding invariance, direction, determinism, save and load hash refusal, layer-wise decay and no-decay rules, layer weights sum to one), test_m5_bundle.py, test_m5_manifest.py (no holdout row in any training set, the fold-file hashes, shortcut AUCs), test_m5_crops.py, test_m5_codecs.py, test_m5_scores.py, test_m5_train.py (smoke runs, resume, the tree-hash refusal), test_augment.py, test_cloud_scripts.py; in test_pipeline.py the identity gate and the live logit's agreement with the scorer.
