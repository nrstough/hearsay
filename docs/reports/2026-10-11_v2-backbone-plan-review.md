1. **Critical — The claimed detection floor is invalid.** The 0.2073 interval belongs to **M1b versus M5 at package-0.5**. Its width does not establish a universal minimum detectable difference for other model pairs, nor justify comparing package-0.3 gains against 0.104. Paired uncertainty depends on which speakers each model gets wrong. Keep H7 executable and compute candidate-specific, paired intervals; do not predeclare its result.

2. **Critical — Stage 1 has unreachable prerequisites.** Its gate requires K5–K18 green, but Step 5 requires K7/K18 on the 1B checkpoint downloaded only in Stage 2. P3a also needs scoring and sample construction that appear later in the execution sequence. Define separate stage-specific gates and move sample declaration, manifest construction and the minimal scorer before the Stage 1 evaluation.

3. **Critical — P2 and P3a turn unsupported performance expectations into correctness assertions.** Documented mean pooling need not outperform alternative pooling on every sample. Likewise, corpus membership does not establish that these exact clips were training examples or guarantee ≤2% EER after this preprocessing. Correct implementations could fail both gates. Verify implementation against an independent reference; treat unexpected accuracy as a diagnostic requiring investigation, with a bounded stop/report outcome.

4. **Critical — The input recipe contradicts the frozen candidate definition.** Step 6 specifies no padding, and Step 9 removes the pad-mode axis, while the spec declares `repeat` primary, `zero`/`whole` diagnostics, and I5/W2 tests for all three. Spectra’s padding lengths are architecture-specific (`src/hearsay/spectra.py`). Amend the candidate definition explicitly and specify the native view’s crop, normalization and minimum-length behavior, including clips shorter than the convolutional receptive field.

5. **Critical — ITW still drives selection despite M6.** Stage 3 exports only the “best candidate” after ITW-based verdicts, and H5 excludes candidates from column recommendations using ITW perturbations. That is selection through aggregate metrics, even without fitting on ITW rows. The handoff and `docs/prod-chunks.md` rule 7 make these populations report-only. Define an allowed selection basis or defer choosing the exported candidate to the sealed benchmark; `provisional: true` alone does not resolve this conflict.

6. **Critical — The verdict rules remain incomplete.** H5 references a “declared margin” without supplying one. There is no complete mapping to `column`/`suppressor`/`drop`, no tie-breaking rule, and no non-regression bands for all three readings as required by v2 rule 5. Freeze those rules before scoring and test boundary cases. Merely recording N also does not account for selecting the strongest of four confidence intervals; specify whether inference is simultaneous or exploratory.

7. **Critical — H11 does not define group-aware uncertainty.** “Stratified bootstrap” could mean resampling clips within generators, which ignores dependence among clips from the same generator or speaker. Specify the resampling units using fold-file groups, the treatment of the single LJ speaker, and identical resamples for candidate and baseline. Distinguish uncertainty conditional on these sampled corpora from uncertainty about unseen generators and speakers.

8. **Critical — The runtime budget omits required inference and lacks enforceable cutoffs.** The 46,000 forwards count one view, but D5/I8 require both native and band-matched scores. Required holdout/test diagnostics and real-weight verification are also absent from that arithmetic. Recalculate the workload after resolving padding and views, and define elapsed-time deadlines that checkpoint, stop and report at each stage. Estimates of 2, 3.75 and potentially 4.2 hours are not themselves time boxes.

9. **Critical — The MPS guard cannot enforce exclusive use.** A one-time `pgrep` check neither blocks execution nor prevents the active sibling lane from starting afterward. Coordinate a machine-wide reservation covering scoring and weight-dependent tests, with an explicit wait/abort path. Also measure peak memory while loading the actual 2B checkpoint: steady-state memory of a randomly initialized model does not cover simultaneous checkpoint tensors and model allocations.

10. **Suggestion — The contamination differential is not an identified inflation estimate.** NSA-versus-ITW differences combine training exposure with corpus difficulty, channel, speakers and generator composition. Subtracting M1b’s corresponding gap does not isolate memorization. Report this as a descriptive generalization-gap difference unless a matched-condition membership control supports the stronger interpretation.

11. **Suggestion — The “77 hermetic tests” claim conflicts with the test definitions.** K15 requires local gitignored weights; K7/K18 require real checkpoints; P2/P3 require labeled audio and measured performance. Loader injection cannot make those checks hermetic. Separate synthetic unit tests from explicitly marked data/weight integration tests, and ensure the break-check worktree can access its required fixtures without downloading or running the entire evaluation.

12. **Suggestion — The rejected 23,790-row basis actually exists.** `splits/nsa_folds_plus_asv19.csv` contains 27,648 total rows and **23,790 non-holdout rows**, exactly as the handoff states. Keeping the NSA-only 16,142 basis is reasonable, but correct the amendment rather than documenting the alternate basis as nonexistent.

13. **Suggestion — Robustness verification leaves named failure modes uncovered.** `m3_probes.perturb()` supplies MP3, noise, speed and shift controls; it does not test telephony or physical replay. The proposed input tests also omit explicit sample-rate, channel, PCM scaling and decoder-padding cases. Add scorer-boundary fixtures through `hearsay.audio.load_audio`, include a telephony cohort, and state physical replay remains untested. Include container and single-speaker dependence in the shortcut audit rather than treating H8’s four continuous features as complete coverage.
