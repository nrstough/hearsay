## Frozen P1

### The problem

A larger pretrained backbone looks like most of what separated HEARSAY from the winning team. Their untuned XLS-R-2B scored In-the-Wild ~0.038 under package-0.3 on their own 2,500-clip sample; our M1b L7 scores 0.193 on our 3,000-clip sample. Not like-for-like, but roughly five times better for a model that is downloaded rather than trained.

The obstacle is telling skill from memory. **Verified against the HF model cards (Oct 11), not assumed:** the AntiDeepfake training table lists 29 corpora totalling 56,370 genuine hours and 18,280 fake hours, and nearly every population we own is in it.

| Our population | In their training table? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| NSA inner + holdout spoofs (DiffSSD) | **yes** | `DiffSSD` listed |
| ASVspoof 2019 LA | **yes**, partition unstated | `ASVspoof2019-LA` listed |
| Local MLAAD cohort (572 clips) | **yes** | `MLAAD` listed |
| LJ Speech reals | **yes** — handoff had this unchecked | `LJSpeech` listed |
| LibriSpeech reals | **effectively** | `LibriTTS`, `LibriTTS-R`, `MLS` listed; same LibriVox source recordings |
| NSA test reals | **likely**, if the VCTK hunch holds | ASVspoof 2019 LA is built from VCTK |
| **In-the-Wild** | **no** | listed as a *test* database, not training |

So every labelled population we own is in-sample for these models except In-the-Wild. Inner and holdout readouts for them are meaningless in the way Spectra's were (B.4). In-the-Wild is a readout only under rule 7.

A second finding from the winner's own evaluation table: fine-tuning made their In-the-Wild clean score **worse** (0.038 zero-shot to 0.060 fine-tuned), helping only under perturbation. That reproduces v1's M5 lesson from independent data, and argues against the GPU path.

### Deltas from the handoff's assumptions, verified at P1

1. **The external drive is mounted** — `/Volumes/Crucial P3 NVME Gen 3 2TB`, 282 GiB free; `data/librispeech` and `data/ljspeech` resolve. The handoff treated the training-audio path as blocked.
2. **Internal disk is 16 GiB free, not the handoff's 27–29.** The four candidates total 17.64 GB of weights, so they do not fit internally at all.
3. **The research backup already exists** — `~/hearsay-v2-research-2026-10-10.tgz`, 33 MB, 01:16 Oct 11. The at-risk item is covered.
4. **Checkpoints are safetensors, not fairseq `.pt`** — but tensor names follow fairseq's module layout, so a strict key-mapped port is still required. A renaming job, not a format conversion.
5. **More candidates exist than the handoff listed** — every family ships an `-nda` (no-data-augmentation) variant, plus `wav2vec-large`, `wav2vec-small`, `hubert-xlarge`.

### Approach

Score the size ladder as-is, no training, on the one population that is not compromised, and report the contamination gap as a number rather than a disclaimer.

1. Inventory each candidate: licence, training corpora, size, run cost.
2. Treat contamination as largely answered by the card and recorded from source; keep one known-member positive control to size the inflation.
3. Score 300M to 1B to 2B on In-the-Wild under all three readings, plus an in-sample population for the differential.
4. Report as a readout with a provisional verdict; hand adoption to the benchmark lane.

Fine-tunes and frozen probes are costed, not run.

### Decisions carried in from the user at P1

- **All four sizes, not one.** The size *trend* is itself the finding; one model cannot show it. Starting at 1.27 GB is a staging order to prove the loader cheaply, not a scope cut.
- **Downloads approved** ("I'm fine with you downloading whatever"), 17.64 GB total, to the external drive.
- **Licence:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, verified on both cards read. Non-commercial **and** share-alike; share-alike reaches anything built on these weights. Recorded as a decision owed before shipping, not resolved here.
- **Time box, two stages.** Stage 1, ~3–4 h: loader + tests + inventory + contamination record + MMS-300M on In-the-Wild. Stage 2, ~3 h MPS: 1B, 1B, 2B, one job at a time. Stop and report at each boundary rather than push through.

### Declared decisions

- **D1 Loader.** Strict transformers port, no fairseq. The 431 = 422 + 7 + 2 budget is asserted, not assumed.
- **D2 Architecture flags.** Read from each repo's shipped `config.json` (`do_stable_layer_norm: true`, `feat_extract_norm: "layer"`, `conv_bias: true`). Never defaulted, never remembered.
- **D3 Positional convolution.** `weight_g`->`original0`, `weight_v`->`original1`, shapes asserted, and the resulting conv weight checked numerically against the weight-norm reconstruction.
- **D4 Head and direction.** `proj_fc` is `[2, hidden]`; the card states logits `[fake, real]`, so `synth_logit = logit[0] - logit[1]`. **This is verified on labelled rows, never assumed**; the direction gate blocks the export and nothing is ever flipped silently (the M3 precedent).
- **D5 Input views.** Two, both recorded: *ours* (`hearsay.embed.prepare_segment`, band-matched) as the primary, for comparability with v1; *native* (the model's own per-clip standardization) recorded beside it.
- **D6 Pooling.** Time-mean over the final encoder hidden state, per the card.
- **D7 Device and dtype.** MPS, fp16 primary; fp32 for the equivalence test. No silent CPU fallback (F5).
- **D8 Weight location.** The external drive, via a redirected HF cache. The internal disk (16 GiB free) is not used for weights (A3).
- **D9 Positive control.** ASVspoof 2019 LA **train/dev only**. The eval partition (A07–A19) is barred from every path (M7). *Rationale: the handoff's step 3 names "ASVspoof 2019 LA" as the control while the shared rules bar LA eval from every readout path. Train and dev share attacks A01–A06 and differ by speaker; all thirteen unseen attacks live in eval. A membership control needs known membership, not unseen generators, so train/dev is the correct choice rather than a compromise. Raised with the user at P2; not overruled.*
- **D10 No calibration on the export.** `score = sigmoid(logit)`, `logit` = the raw margin (the Spectra D6 precedent). Platt stays a diagnostic if used at all.
- **D11 Pre-declared candidate set.** Frozen below before any score is read; the comparison count N is recorded with the verdict (H1, H2).
- **D12 Provisional verdict.** Every verdict carries `provisional: true` until a sealed population outside the AntiDeepfake training list exists (rule 7, M9).

### Pass rules

- A candidate is **not** called an improvement unless its In-the-Wild gain over the baseline clears the bootstrap 5th percentile above zero (H3).
- A candidate whose clean-to-perturbed degradation exceeds the baseline's by the declared margin is **flagged** `condition_overfit` (H5). **Amended per Codex C5: the flag is descriptive and does not exclude.** Excluding a candidate on In-the-Wild perturbations is selection on In-the-Wild, which rule 7 bars however it is phrased, and `provisional: true` does not cure it. This lane ranks nothing on In-the-Wild; which candidate (if any) is adopted belongs to the benchmark lane and its sealed population.
